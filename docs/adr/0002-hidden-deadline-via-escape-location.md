# Hidden Deadline via Escape Location

Cases have a real time limit, but it is never shown to the player as an explicit deadline. Instead, the Fugitive Route ends at an unnamed, unreachable Escape Location — when the fugitive reaches it, the trail goes cold and the Case is lost. The player experiences time pressure through clue staleness (e.g., "was seen here two days ago") and the In-Game Clock, not through a countdown timer.

This avoids the artificial feel of "solve the case before Sunday 5pm" while preserving the mechanical tension of a deadline. The trade-off is that players cannot calculate exactly how much time remains — this is intentional.
